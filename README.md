# PrepSec+

A study hub for the **CompTIA Security+ (SY0-701)** exam. Practice quizzes, timed full exams, hands-on PBQ labs, flashcards, a study guide, and domain-tagged notes, with your progress saved to your account so it follows you from phone to laptop.

**Live app:** https://kdwills9.github.io/PrepSec-Plus/

## Features

- **Dashboard:** Overall accuracy, an estimated exam score out of 900, and counts for mastered flashcards, solved PBQ labs, and saved notes.
- **Weighted domain proficiency:** Your accuracy in each of the five SY0-701 domains, shown against the official exam weighting and the 80% target.
- **Adaptive drills:** A missed-question queue with spaced-repetition review, plus a one-click drill for your weakest domain.
- **Practice quiz:** A built-in bank of 500+ questions across all five domains.
- **Full exams:** Ten timed full-length exams, plus a 90-minute exam mode.
- **PBQ labs:** 27 performance-based simulations for hands-on practice.
- **Flashcards:** 212 definition-first flashcards with mastery tracking.
- **Study guide:** A built-in guide organized by exam domain.
- **My Notes:** Highlight text and save notes, auto-tagged by domain.
- **Cloud sync:** Sign in and your progress and notes stay in sync across your devices.
- **Settings:** Account and profile controls.
- **Demo profile:** Explore the app with sample progress. Nothing in demo mode is saved or synced.

## Getting started

1. Open the live app in any modern browser.
2. Sign in to save your progress, or try a demo profile first.
3. Start from the Dashboard and pick a quiz, exam, lab, or flashcard set.

To install it as an app, use your browser's install option, or on iPhone and iPad open it in Safari, tap Share, then Add to Home Screen.

## Your data

When you are signed in, your progress and notes are saved to your account and sync across your devices. Demo profiles are never saved.

## Built with

- A single-page HTML, CSS, and JavaScript app, with no build step
- Firebase for sign-in and cloud storage
- GitHub Pages for hosting
- A web app manifest and service worker, so the app is installable

## Roadmap

Planned work:

- **Notebooks:** Notes grouped by exam domain, then split into notebooks by where they were created (Practice Quiz, Study Guide, Flashcards, PBQ Labs), with a click-through back to the source question or study guide section.
- **Quizzes and timers:** An 8-question quick quiz, a 45-question Mini Mock (half the size of the real exam), and one continuous countdown in place of per-question timers.
- **More content:** A deeper study guide, a larger question pool built into the app, a larger flashcard set, and PBQ labs that match the real exam format as closely as possible.
- **Question variations:** Several differently framed questions per concept, shuffled answer order, and spaced repetition by concept.
- **Bug fix:** The highlight note popup reopening after Save Highlight.
